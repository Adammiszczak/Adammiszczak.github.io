## How I work

I connect technical evidence to business priorities. That means investigating how search systems encounter a site, identifying issues that matter across large catalogs, and explaining practical next steps to SEO, product and engineering teams. I also use programming and automation to make research and repeatable analysis more efficient.

## Background

My earlier work includes SEO tools, browser extensions, JavaScript and Java projects, data collection and workflow automation. Those projects remain part of this site’s [history archive](/history/); they support my technical perspective, while my current focus is enterprise SEO, GEO and AI Search.

I publish ongoing notes and analysis on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adam-miszczak/) and [Substack](https://substack.com/@adammiszczak/). For technical writing, see [Articles](/articles/).
